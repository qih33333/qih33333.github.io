Research

Statistical and machine-learning methods for longitudinal and biomedical data.

Feb 2026 - Present Rice University

Longitudinal Variational Autoencoder for Within-Subject Image Prediction

Supervised by Dr. Huixia Judy Wang

  • Developed a longitudinal variational autoencoder with a mixed-effects latent backbone and a first-order stochastic transition to model subject-specific temporal dynamics in image sequences.
  • Reformulated the task as prefix-conditioned missing-visit prediction under a sequential latent-variable framework.
  • Derived an ELBO-based objective and evaluated one-step and recursive forecasting performance on longitudinal imaging data.

Variational inference · Longitudinal imaging · Mixed effects

Feb - Jul 2025 Rice & BCM

A Novel Hemodynamic Measure to Assess Risk of Acute Kidney Injury After Cardiac Surgery

Supervised by Dr. Meng Li

  • Applied univariate and multivariable logistic regression to quantify the association between tissue perfusion pressure and postoperative acute kidney injury.
  • Identified a 38 mm Hg threshold using regression-based risk estimation and evaluated its independence after statistical adjustment.
  • Conducted sensitivity and subgroup analyses stratified by vasoactive inotrope score.

Logistic regression · Risk estimation · Clinical data

Jan - Jun 2026 Rice University

Dynamic Machine Learning for Acute Kidney Injury Prediction

Research Assistant with Dr. Meng Li

  • Developed rolling-window features from hemodynamics, laboratory measurements, medications, and clinical data.
  • Evaluated XGBoost, random forest, and logistic regression under patient-level data splits.
  • Built calibration-based evaluation metrics and conducted external validation using the MIMIC dataset.

Machine learning · Calibration · External validation
