Hao Qi

Ph.D. Student in Applied Mathematics Statistics Concentration

I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics (Statistics concentration) at UNC Charlotte, advised by Dr. Qingning Zhou. My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical learning, time-series analysis, and biostatistics.

Before joining UNC Charlotte, I completed the Master of Statistics at Rice University, where I worked on longitudinal image prediction and acute kidney injury research with Dr. Huixia Judy Wang and Dr. Meng Li. I also earned B.S. degrees in Mathematics from the University of North Carolina Wilmington and in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Chongqing University of Arts and Sciences.

I am particularly interested in developing statistical and machine-learning methods for healthcare applications using real-world medical data, including clinical measurements and physiological time series. My previous work includes postoperative risk prediction, longitudinal modeling, and forecasting for financial and small-sample time-series data.

Research interests

Statistical learning Longitudinal data analysis Biostatistics Time-series forecasting Biomedical machine learning
